## How to run a mock with Claude
- Say: "Mock technical round, topic X, one question at a time, feedback after each."
- Answer aloud first, then type your answer.
- Ask for: what was right, what was missing, a tighter version in 60 seconds.

## Rotation
- Day 1: STA and synthesis.
- Day 2: equivalent transforms, LEC, low power.
- Day 3: PD flow and scenarios.
- Day 4: resume deep dives and behavioral.
- Day 5: CoderPad drills timed.

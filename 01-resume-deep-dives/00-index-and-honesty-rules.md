## Rules for every project file
- Say what the scaffold or team provided versus what you personally did.
- Use real numbers only. No invented metrics. Mark unknowns `[VERIFY]`.
- Be ready to go three levels deep: what, why, how, then what failed.
- Hiring managers pick one item and keep asking "why". Know your top 3 cold:
  RV32I CPU, DV testbench, PD onboarding.
- Avoid inflated language such as "resolved over 12 timing issues" unless the count
  is exact and you can list them.

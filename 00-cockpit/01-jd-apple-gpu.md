Status: reviewed
Priority: high

---

## Title
GPU Implementation Internship (Austin, TX, January to August 2027)

## Core responsibilities
- Optimize designs to achieve aggressive power, area, timing goals.
- Deliver timing clean, logically equivalent netlists to the physical design team.
- Collaborate effectively with different functional teams.

## Qualifications
- Exposure to front-end design synthesis and large chip integration.
- Knowledge of advanced synthesis techniques for aggressive low power, area, timing.
- Basic understanding of physical design: floorplanning, placement, congestion,
  setup/hold timing closure.
- Fast learner, highly motivated, solves technical challenges using innovation.
- Strong written and verbal communication, collaborates across functional teams.
- Familiarity with static timing analysis, Verilog/VHDL, formal verification.
- With some guidance, can optimize designs for low power and high performance using
  logically equivalent RTL transforms.
- ECO implementation experience is a plus.
- Familiarity with simulation and debugging tools.
- Knowledge of DFT insertion and multi-mode timing constraints.
- Previous internship on CPUs, GPUs or DSPs desirable.

## Education line in posting
MSEE, or BSEE with relevant industry experience. (Intern posting: expect fundamentals
and reasoning, not tool mastery.)
